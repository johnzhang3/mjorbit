\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

This section describes the equations integrated by \mjorbit{}, the frames in
which they live, and how the chief-orbit propagator and the \mj{}
multibody/contact solver are coupled in a single step. We begin with the
standard inertial equations of orbit mechanics, then derive the
chief-centered equations that \mj{} actually integrates, and finally
specify the force model and coupling architecture.

\subsection{Notation}
\label{subsec:notation}

We use the following symbols throughout. SI units are used inside \mj{};
the orbital layer uses \si{km}, \si{km/s}, \si{km/s\squared}.

\begin{description}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
  \item[$\bm R \in \R^3$] Chief reference position in the Earth-centered
    inertial (ECI) frame, in km.
  \item[$\bm V \in \R^3$] Chief reference velocity in ECI, in \si{km/s}.
  \item[$t \in \R$] Mission elapsed time, in s.
  \item[$\bm r_i \in \R^3$] Position of body $i$'s center of mass in the
    \mj{} world frame (chief-centered, ECI-parallel axes), in m.
  \item[$\bm v_i \in \R^3$] Linear velocity of body $i$'s COM in the world
    frame, in \si{m/s}.
  \item[$\bm\omega_i \in \R^3$] Angular velocity of body $i$, expressed in
    the body frame, in \si{rad/s}.
  \item[$\bm R_i = \bm R + 10^{-3}\bm r_i$] Absolute ECI position of body
    $i$, in km.
  \item[$m_i$] Mass of body $i$, in kg.
  \item[$J_i \in \R^{3\times 3}$] Inertia tensor of body $i$ about its COM,
    in body frame, in \si{kg.m\squared}.
  \item[$\mu = GM_\oplus$] Earth standard gravitational parameter,
    \SI{398600.4418}{km^3/s^2}.
  \item[$R_\oplus$] Earth equatorial radius, \SI{6378.137}{km}.
  \item[$J_2$] Earth second zonal coefficient, $1.082626\times 10^{-3}$.
  \item[$\bm B(\bm R, t)$] Geomagnetic flux density at $\bm R$, in T.
  \item[$\rho(\bm R)$] Atmospheric mass density at $\bm R$, in
    \si{kg/m^3}.
  \item[$\bm\omega_\oplus$] Earth angular velocity vector in ECI,
    $\bm\omega_\oplus = (0, 0, \omega_\oplus)$.
  \item[$C_{IL}, C_{LI}$] Direction cosine matrices ECI$\leftrightarrow$LVLH;
    $C_{LI}$ maps ECI vectors to LVLH coordinates and
    $C_{IL} = C_{LI}^\top$.
  \item[$R_i^{wb}$] Rotation matrix world-from-body for body $i$ (\mj{}
    \texttt{xmat}); $R_i^{wp}$ is world-from-principal-axes (\mj{}
    \texttt{ximat}).
\end{description}

We denote the absolute ECI velocity of body $i$ by
$\bm V_i = \bm V + 10^{-3}\bm v_i$.

\subsection{Frames}
\label{subsec:frames}

Three frames are used.

\paragraph{ECI ($\mathcal{F}_I$)} A non-rotating geocentric inertial frame.
The chief reference state $(\bm R, \bm V, t)$ and all environmental fields
($\bm B$, $\rho$, sun direction $\hat{\bm s}$) are expressed here.

\paragraph{World ($\mathcal{F}_W$)} A chief-centered, ECI-axis-parallel
frame. Its origin follows $\bm R(t)$, its axes are parallel to ECI axes,
and consequently its angular velocity in $\mathcal{F}_I$ is zero. This is
the frame in which \mj{} represents $\bm r_i$, $\bm v_i$, $R_i^{wb}$, and
all applied wrenches. $\mathcal{F}_W$ is non-inertial because its origin
accelerates, but it is non-rotating: only a translational pseudo-force
appears in it (Section~\ref{subsec:chief-frame-eom}).

\paragraph{LVLH ($\mathcal{F}_L$)} A chief-centered rotating frame defined
by
\begin{equation}
  \hat{\bm x}_L = \frac{\bm R}{\|\bm R\|},\quad
  \hat{\bm z}_L = \frac{\bm R\times\bm V}{\|\bm R\times\bm V\|},\quad
  \hat{\bm y}_L = \hat{\bm z}_L\times\hat{\bm x}_L,
  \label{eq:lvlh-basis}
\end{equation}
with angular velocity (in LVLH coordinates)
\begin{equation}
  \bm\omega_L \;=\; C_{LI}\frac{\bm R\times\bm V}{\|\bm R\|^2}.
  \label{eq:omega-lvlh}
\end{equation}
$\mathcal{F}_L$ is a derived frame used for analysis and IO; it is not the
frame in which \mj{} integrates.

\subsection{Orbit Mechanics: Inertial Equations}
\label{subsec:orbit-eom}

For a point mass at ECI position $\bm R$, the Newtonian equation of motion
under Earth's gravity field plus arbitrary perturbations is
\begin{equation}
  \ddot{\bm R} \;=\; \bm g(\bm R) + \bm a^{\text{pert}}(\bm R, \bm V, t),
  \label{eq:point-mass-eom}
\end{equation}
where $\bm g(\bm R)$ is the gravitational acceleration and
$\bm a^{\text{pert}}$ aggregates non-gravitational perturbations.

\paragraph{Two-body gravity}
\begin{equation}
  \bm g_{2B}(\bm R) \;=\; -\frac{\mu}{\|\bm R\|^3}\,\bm R.
  \label{eq:two-body}
\end{equation}

\paragraph{$J_2$ correction} The dominant zonal harmonic adds
\begin{equation}
  \bm g_{J_2}(\bm R) \;=\;
  -\frac{3}{2}\,\mu\,J_2\,\frac{R_\oplus^2}{\|\bm R\|^5}
  \begin{bmatrix}
    \bigl(1 - 5(R_z/\|\bm R\|)^2\bigr)\,R_x \\
    \bigl(1 - 5(R_z/\|\bm R\|)^2\bigr)\,R_y \\
    \bigl(3 - 5(R_z/\|\bm R\|)^2\bigr)\,R_z
  \end{bmatrix},
  \label{eq:j2}
\end{equation}
so the gravity model used by \mjorbit{} is
$\bm g(\bm R) = \bm g_{2B}(\bm R) + \bm g_{J_2}(\bm R)$ when $J_2$ is
enabled, and $\bm g_{2B}(\bm R)$ otherwise.

\subsection{Chief Orbit Propagation}
\label{subsec:chief-prop}

The chief is integrated as a point mass under gravity plus a swarm-averaged
non-gravity feedback acceleration $\bm a_\text{fb}$ (defined in
Section~\ref{subsec:swarm-feedback}):
\begin{equation}
  \frac{d}{dt}
  \begin{bmatrix}\bm R\\\bm V\end{bmatrix}
  =
  \begin{bmatrix}\bm V\\\bm g(\bm R) + \bm a_\text{fb}\end{bmatrix}.
  \label{eq:chief-ivp}
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:chief-ivp} is integrated by classical fourth-order
Runge--Kutta with step size $\Delta t_o$ (default: equal to the \mj{}
timestep $\Delta t$),
\begin{align}
  \bm k_1 &= \bm f(\bm R, \bm V), \\
  \bm k_2 &= \bm f\!\left(\bm R + \tfrac{\Delta t_o}{2}\bm k_1^R,\;
                          \bm V + \tfrac{\Delta t_o}{2}\bm k_1^V\right), \\
  \bm k_3 &= \bm f\!\left(\bm R + \tfrac{\Delta t_o}{2}\bm k_2^R,\;
                          \bm V + \tfrac{\Delta t_o}{2}\bm k_2^V\right), \\
  \bm k_4 &= \bm f\!\left(\bm R + \Delta t_o\bm k_3^R,\;
                          \bm V + \Delta t_o\bm k_3^V\right), \\
  \begin{bmatrix}\bm R\\\bm V\end{bmatrix}_{n+1}
   &=
  \begin{bmatrix}\bm R\\\bm V\end{bmatrix}_n
   + \tfrac{\Delta t_o}{6}\bigl(\bm k_1 + 2\bm k_2 + 2\bm k_3 + \bm k_4\bigr).
  \label{eq:rk4}
\end{align}
Here $\bm f(\bm R,\bm V)=(\bm V,\,\bm g(\bm R)+\bm a_\text{fb})$ packs the
right-hand side of~\eqref{eq:chief-ivp}. The chief acceleration appearing
later in the chief-frame equations is therefore
\begin{equation}
  \bm a_\text{chief} \;\equiv\; \ddot{\bm R} \;=\; \bm g(\bm R) + \bm a_\text{fb}.
  \label{eq:a-chief}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Multi-Body Equations of Motion in the Chief Frame}
\label{subsec:chief-frame-eom}

Let body $i$ have absolute position
$\bm R_i = \bm R + 10^{-3}\bm r_i$. Newton in $\mathcal{F}_I$ reads
\begin{equation}
  m_i \ddot{\bm R}_i \;=\; m_i\,\bm g(\bm R_i) + \bm F_i^{\text{ng}},
  \label{eq:newton-eci}
\end{equation}
with $\bm F_i^{\text{ng}}$ the non-gravity force on body $i$. Substituting
$\bm R_i = \bm R + 10^{-3}\bm r_i$ and rearranging,
\begin{equation}
  m_i\,10^{-3}\ddot{\bm r}_i \;=\;
    m_i\bigl[\bm g(\bm R_i) - \bm a_\text{chief}\bigr]
    + \bm F_i^{\text{ng}}.
  \label{eq:chief-frame-eom-eci-units}
\end{equation}
Converting the $\bm g$-difference from \si{km/s\squared} to \si{m/s\squared}
($\times 10^{3}$) and dividing both sides by $10^{-3}$,
\begin{equation}
  m_i\ddot{\bm r}_i \;=\;
    \underbrace{m_i\bigl[\tilde{\bm g}(\bm R_i)-\tilde{\bm g}(\bm R)\bigr]}_{\text{differential gravity}}
    \;-\;
    \underbrace{m_i\,\tilde{\bm a}_\text{fb}}_{\text{origin compensation}}
    \;+\;
    \bm F_i^{\text{ng}},
  \label{eq:chief-frame-eom}
\end{equation}
where tildes denote SI quantities ($\tilde{\bm g} \equiv 10^3\,\bm g$,
$\tilde{\bm a}_\text{fb}\equiv 10^3\,\bm a_\text{fb}$).
Equation~\eqref{eq:chief-frame-eom} is the equation of motion that \mj{}
integrates per body. Because $\mathcal{F}_W$ is non-rotating, no Coriolis,
centrifugal, or Euler terms appear; the only fictitious force is the
translational $-m_i\bm a_\text{chief}$, which is split into the gravity
piece $-m_i\bm g(\bm R)$ (absorbed into the differential gravity term) and
the non-gravity piece $-m_i\bm a_\text{fb}$ (the origin compensation).

\subsubsection{Per-body external force model}
\label{subsubsec:force-model}

The non-gravity force $\bm F_i^{\text{ng}}$ is the sum of all environmental
and actuator contributions, evaluated at body $i$'s own state:
\begin{equation}
  \bm F_i^{\text{ng}}
   \;=\;
   \bm F_i^{\text{drag}} + \bm F_i^{\text{SRP}}
   + \bm F_i^{\text{thr}}.
  \label{eq:f-non-grav}
\end{equation}
Magnetic, magnetorquer, RW, CMG, and gravity-gradient effects produce
torques only and are summarised below.

\paragraph{Drag} For each surface $k$ on body $i$ with body-frame normal
$\hat{\bm n}_k$, area $A_k$, drag coefficient $C_d^k$, and center of pressure
$\bm p_k$, denote the surface point ECI position and velocity by
$\bm R_{ik} = \bm R + 10^{-3}(\bm r_i + R_i^{wb}\bm p_k)$ and
$\bm V_{ik} = \bm V + 10^{-3}(\bm v_i + R_i^{wb}(\bm\omega_i\times\bm p_k))$.
The atmosphere co-rotates with Earth, so the relative velocity at the surface
is
\begin{equation}
  \bm V_{ik}^{\text{rel}} \;=\; \bm V_{ik} - \bm\omega_\oplus\times\bm R_{ik},
\end{equation}
and the drag force in world coordinates is
\begin{equation}
  \bm F_{ik}^{\text{drag}} = -\tfrac{1}{2}\,\rho(\bm R)\,C_d^k\,A_k\,
   \max(0,\hat{\bm n}_k^w\!\cdot\hat{\bm V}^{\text{rel}})\,
   \|\bm V_{ik}^{\text{rel}}\|\,\bm V_{ik}^{\text{rel}},
  \label{eq:drag}
\end{equation}
with $\hat{\bm n}_k^w = R_i^{wb}\hat{\bm n}_k$,
$\hat{\bm V}^{\text{rel}}=\bm V_{ik}^{\text{rel}}/\|\bm V_{ik}^{\text{rel}}\|$
(positive only on the windward face). The accompanying torque about body
$i$'s COM is
\begin{equation}
  \bm\tau_{ik}^{\text{drag}} = (R_i^{wb}\bm p_k)\times\bm F_{ik}^{\text{drag}}.
\end{equation}
$\rho(\bm R)$ is currently evaluated at the chief position only and shared
across surfaces; per-body velocity is the dominant term for short-baseline
swarms.

\paragraph{Solar radiation pressure} For an unshadowed surface
($\eta(\bm R)>0$, with eclipse factor $\eta\in\{0,1\}$),
\begin{equation}
  \bm F_{ik}^{\text{SRP}} = -\eta\,P_{\!\odot}\,C_r^k\,A_k\,
   \max(0,\hat{\bm n}_k^w\!\cdot\hat{\bm s})\,\hat{\bm s},
  \label{eq:srp}
\end{equation}
where $P_{\!\odot} = \SI{4.56e-6}{N/m^2}$ is the solar pressure at 1\,AU and
$\hat{\bm s}$ is the Sun unit vector in ECI.

\paragraph{Residual magnetic dipole} A body-frame residual dipole
$\bm m_i^b$ produces
\begin{equation}
  \bm\tau_i^{\text{mag}} = R_i^{wb}\bigl(\bm m_i^b\times R_i^{bw}\bm B(\bm R)\bigr),
  \label{eq:res-mag}
\end{equation}
with $R_i^{bw}=(R_i^{wb})^\top$. $\bm B$ is a tilted dipole evaluated at the
chief position.

\paragraph{Magnetorquers} Each magnetorquer commands a body-frame dipole
$\bm m_i^{b,\text{cmd}}$ subject to a magnitude limit $m_{\max}$, producing
$\bm\tau = \bm m^{b,\text{cmd}}\times\bm B^b$ in the body frame and
rotated to world.

\paragraph{Gravity-gradient torque} The differential-gravity force in
\eqref{eq:chief-frame-eom} is evaluated at body $i$'s COM and therefore
produces no net torque on a single rigid body. The analytic torque from
integrating gravity over the body's mass distribution, in the small-extent
limit $\|\bm r_i\|\ll\|\bm R_i\|$, is
\begin{equation}
  \bm\tau_i^{gg}
    = \frac{3\mu}{\|\bm R_i\|^3}\,
      \hat{\bm R}_i \times \bigl(J_i^w\hat{\bm R}_i\bigr),
  \quad
  \hat{\bm R}_i = \bm R_i / \|\bm R_i\|,
  \label{eq:gg-torque}
\end{equation}
where $J_i^w = R_i^{wp}\,\mathrm{diag}(I_{i,1},I_{i,2},I_{i,3})\,(R_i^{wp})^\top$
is the inertia tensor in world axes built from the principal moments and
the world-from-principal-axes rotation $R_i^{wp}$ (\mj{} \texttt{ximat}).

\paragraph{Reaction wheels} For wheel $j$ with axis $\hat{\bm a}_j^b$,
inertia $I_j$, and signed wheel speed $\Omega_j$, the wheel angular
momentum in the body frame is $\bm h_j^b = I_j\Omega_j\hat{\bm a}_j^b$. The
torque on the host body is the gyroscopic reaction plus a commanded
acceleration $\alpha_j$:
\begin{equation}
  \bm\tau_j^{rw,b}
    = -\bm\omega_i\times\bm h_j^b
      \;-\; I_j\,\alpha_j\,\hat{\bm a}_j^b,
  \label{eq:rw}
\end{equation}
with $\alpha_j = \tau_j^{\text{cmd}}/I_j$ saturated by torque/speed limits.
The wheel speed is integrated separately by Euler:
$\Omega_j^{n+1} = \Omega_j^{n} + \alpha_j\,\Delta t$.

\paragraph{Single-gimbal CMG} With gimbal axis $\hat{\bm g}^b$, initial
spin axis $\hat{\bm s}^b(0)$, rotor momentum $h$, and gimbal angle
$\theta_j$,
\begin{equation}
  \hat{\bm s}^b(\theta) = \cos\theta\,\hat{\bm s}^b(0)
    + \sin\theta\,\hat{\bm g}^b\times\hat{\bm s}^b(0),
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
  \bm\tau_j^{cmg,b} = -\bm\omega_i\times\bigl(h\,\hat{\bm s}^b(\theta)\bigr)
    - h\,\dot\theta_j\,\hat{\bm g}^b\times\hat{\bm s}^b(\theta),
  \label{eq:cmg}
\end{equation}
with gimbal angle integrated by Euler under rate and angle limits.

\paragraph{Thrusters} A thruster on body $i$ at body-frame position
$\bm p^b$ along direction $\hat{\bm d}^b$ with commanded force
$F_j\in[0,F_{\max}]$ produces world-frame force and torque
\begin{equation}
  \bm F_j^{w} = R_i^{wb}\,F_j\hat{\bm d}^b,\quad
  \bm\tau_j^{w} = R_i^{wb}\bigl((\bm p^b - \bm p^{b,com})\times F_j\hat{\bm d}^b\bigr).
  \label{eq:thr}
\end{equation}

\subsubsection{Swarm-averaged feedback}
\label{subsec:swarm-feedback}

The non-gravity translational force on body $i$ contributes to a single
shared feedback acceleration that drives the chief reference. Writing
$M = \sum_i m_i$, define
\begin{equation}
  \tilde{\bm a}_\text{fb}
    \;=\; \frac{1}{M}\sum_i\bm F_i^{\text{ng,trans}},
  \label{eq:a-feedback}
\end{equation}
where $\bm F_i^{\text{ng,trans}}$ is the translational portion of
$\bm F_i^{\text{ng}}$ (drag, SRP, thrust). Converting to \si{km/s\squared}
gives the $\bm a_\text{fb}$ used in~\eqref{eq:chief-ivp}. The same
$\tilde{\bm a}_\text{fb}$ is subtracted from each body
in~\eqref{eq:chief-frame-eom} as the origin compensation $-m_i\tilde{\bm a}_\text{fb}$.
This couples the chief reference to the swarm centroid without altering
relative motion: any choice of chief trajectory yields the same $\bm r_i(t)$,
because~\eqref{eq:chief-frame-eom} is invariant under
$\bm a_\text{chief}\to\bm a_\text{chief}+\bm a^*$ when the same $\bm a^*$ is
applied to all bodies. The swarm-mean choice keeps $\|\bm r_i\|$ small.

\subsection{\mj{} Multibody and Contact Solver}
\label{subsec:mj-solver}

Inside $\mathcal{F}_W$, \mj{} treats $(\bm r_i,\dot{\bm r}_i,\bm q^{joint},
\dot{\bm q}^{joint})$ as a generalised state and integrates the standard
articulated-body equations
\begin{equation}
  M(q)\ddot q + \bm c(q,\dot q) \;=\; \bm\tau^{\text{passive}} +
   \bm\tau^{\text{ctrl}} + J_c^\top\bm\lambda,
  \label{eq:mj-eom}
\end{equation}
where $M$ is the joint-space mass matrix, $\bm c$ collects Coriolis,
centripetal, and gyroscopic terms internal to the multibody chain (i.e.\
those generated by relative motion of links, not by frame rotation),
$\bm\tau^{\text{passive}}$ is the passive force vector (\texttt{qfrc\_passive}),
$\bm\tau^{\text{ctrl}}$ is from \mj{} actuators, and $J_c^\top\bm\lambda$ is
the contact constraint impulse computed by \mj{}'s convex contact solver.
\mj{}'s built-in uniform gravity is disabled
($\texttt{opt.gravity}=\bm 0$) so that the gravity model
in~\eqref{eq:chief-frame-eom} is not duplicated.

\mjorbit{} writes the per-body wrench
$\bigl(\bm F_i^{\text{tot}},\,\bm\tau_i^{\text{tot}}\bigr)$ from
Section~\ref{subsubsec:force-model} into $\bm\tau^{\text{passive}}$ via
$\texttt{mj\_applyFT}$ at each body's COM. \mj{} is otherwise responsible
for: integrating $(q,\dot q)$, enforcing joint and equality constraints,
detecting contacts, and computing contact impulses $\bm\lambda$.

\subsection{Coupling Architecture}
\label{subsec:coupling}

The chief propagator and \mj{} are coupled through a \mj{} plugin
(\texttt{mujoco\_orbit.orbit}) that owns the chief state, environment
caches, actuator state, and per-body wrench buffer. The plugin registers
two capability hooks: \texttt{mjPLUGIN\_PASSIVE} and a per-step
\texttt{Advance} callback. A single call to \texttt{mjo\_step} executes
the following stages.

\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
  \item \textbf{Cache refresh.} Recompute LVLH bases
    $C_{IL}, C_{LI}, \bm\omega_L, \dot{\bm\omega}_L$ from $(\bm R,\bm V)$
    using~\eqref{eq:lvlh-basis}--\eqref{eq:omega-lvlh} (with
    $\dot{\bm\omega}_L$ derived from $\bm g(\bm R)$ including $J_2$ when
    enabled). Recompute $\hat{\bm s}(t)$, $\eta(\bm R, t)$,
    $\bm B(\bm R, t)$, $\rho(\bm R)$.
  \item \textbf{Force assembly} (\texttt{Compute}, passive stage). Evaluate
    \eqref{eq:chief-frame-eom}\,--\,\eqref{eq:thr} per body, accumulating
    into $\texttt{qfrc\_passive}$ via \texttt{mj\_applyFT} and into a Python
    visible buffer.
  \item \textbf{Multibody integration.} \mj{} solves~\eqref{eq:mj-eom} and
    advances $(q,\dot q)$ by $\Delta t$ using its semi-implicit Euler or
    RK4 integrator (configurable in the XML).
  \item \textbf{Actuator advance} (\texttt{Advance}). Euler-integrate
    reaction-wheel speeds $\Omega_j$ and CMG gimbal angles $\theta_j$ under
    saturation limits.
  \item \textbf{Chief advance} (\texttt{Advance}). Compute $\bm a_\text{fb}$
    from accumulated swarm forces (\ref{eq:a-feedback}), then integrate
    $(\bm R,\bm V)$ over $\Delta t_o$ using
    RK4~\eqref{eq:rk4} on~\eqref{eq:chief-ivp}.
  \item \textbf{Cache refresh.} Re-sync caches at the new $(\bm R,\bm V)$
    so subsequent reads of \texttt{data.frame} and \texttt{data.env} are
    consistent with the post-step chief state.
\end{enumerate}

\mj{}'s contact solver receives no special treatment: contacts are
resolved in $\mathcal{F}_W$, where the only forces are the per-body wrenches
above and \mj{}'s internal joint and constraint forces. Contact impulses
$\bm\lambda$ do not feed back into the chief orbit
($\bm a_\text{fb}$ uses external translational forces only, not contact
impulses), preserving the interpretation of the chief as a free-flying
reference.
